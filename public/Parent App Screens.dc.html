Parent App

Five screens for the school bus parent app. Every phone is live: tap tabs, open a child, mark days, open alerts.

01Children
Each child with today's pick-up and drop-off status, and a delay banner when the bus runs late.
02Bus Information
Bus, route, times and crew for each trip. Pick-up and drop-off can use different buses.
03Live Tracking
Live bus position, ETA at the child's stop, and the crew on board. Other children's stops stay private.
04Delay Alerts
Delays, boarding and drop-off alerts. Trip alerts open the live map.
05Bus Not Required
Parents pick the days a child is away or sick. The driver and bus attendant are notified.
06Authorised Guardians
Choose who may receive your child at drop-off. The attendant checks name and photo at handover.
