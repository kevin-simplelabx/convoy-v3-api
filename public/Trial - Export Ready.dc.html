Route Plans
Welcome to Route Plans
Route engine powered by Convoy Pathfinder
The routing engine turns weeks of route planning into days. It works like a calculator: it follows a fixed set of rules, mixed with variables and a degree of randomness, and tests millions of combinations to find the best routes mathematically. Think of it as a fast, tireless planning partner that gets you most of the way there, so you can spend your time on the decisions that need a human.
Tip 1 of 4
How it works
  • Every run explores many possible routes and keeps the best one
  • Results can differ slightly between runs because of randomness
    • Run it again to get another option
    • Use Compare Plans to pick the best one
Tip 2 of 4
What affects the results
  • Map data comes from third parties and may not be fully accurate
  • Your setup and the real world
    • Available bus sizes and starting locations
    • Road conditions and driver habits
    • Your clustering preferences
  • Missing or wrong rider addresses and postal codes lead to poor routes. Check rider data first
Tip 3 of 4
Always review the plan
  • Check every route before using it
  • Edit by hand where needed
    • Move riders and stops
    • Reorder or reroute
    • Change bus sizes
  • A plan stays a draft until you choose Make Current
Tip 4 of 4
A head start, not the finish line
  • The engine gives you a strong starting point
  • Your local knowledge does the rest and makes the routes right for your riders
The Technology
Powered by Convoy Pathfinder
  • Built for school transport: bell times, ride time limits, bus capacity and attendants
  • Plans with real road travel times, not straight lines
  • Combines proven optimisation methods and keeps improving until no better plan is found
  • Choose how hard it works, from a quick Low run to a thorough Max run

How Max Sector Width Works
{{ sectorSvg }}
Picture a clock face around the school. Each bus should cover one slice of it, so it heads out in one direction and doesn't zig-zag across town.
Far route (about 10 km): its stops must fit inside the Max Sector Width, 40° by default.
Nearer route (about 5 km): may spread wider, here up to 80°. Close to school, stops that look far apart in angle are only a short drive apart. Allowed up to 3× the limit.
Too wide: stops spread over 120° at 10 km would need one bus to sweep across three directions. The engine splits them onto separate buses, shown in purple and blue.
Stops within Sector Ignores Within (2 km) don't count, because every direction meets at the school gate. Set Max Sector Width to 0 to turn the rule off.
